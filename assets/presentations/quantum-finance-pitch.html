1. The Challenge | The Burning Pension Gap
Money sitting in savings accounts loses purchasing power-yet entering the stock market terrifies savers
THE RETIREMENT BURNING PLATFORM
The Inflation Reality: €100 stored in a low-interest bank account in 2026 shrinks to ~€75 by 2035, and less than ~€45 by 2050 in real purchasing power. Traditional cash savings cannot fund retirement.
The State's Solution: The German government is launching the "Gesetzliche Kapitalrente"-investing state pension capital directly into the stock market to capture productivity growth.
The Public Backlash: Citizens fear market crashes. The state cannot simply buy risky assets and pray; savers demand non-negotiable downside safety rules.
THE QUESTIONS WE MUST SOLVE
1. Can we add technology safely? Management proposes adding tech stocks to boost returns. Does the reward justify the risk?
2. Can we enforce hard safety rules? Pension law requires that monthly losses must not exceed legal limits more than 10% of the time.
3. What about passive non-choosers? Millions of citizens never make an active choice and default to Balanced. How do we protect them?
Key Takeaway: Public pension funds cannot gamble: any active sector shift requires affirmative proof of safety across all market stress tests.

Standard quantum algorithms propose illegal baskets; our circuit is physically locked into legal portfolios
HARDWARE-ENFORCED GUARDRAILS

What Quantum Enabled: 100% of measured shots are legally valid portfolios. Zero invalid measurement waste.
Key Takeaway: By building legal guardrails directly into quantum physics, our algorithm physically cannot propose an illegal portfolio.
